# Merchant interviews and task testing

Status: NOT YET CONDUCTED. Do not fill observations with invented participant quotes.

## Two 20-minute interviews

1. Ask for the last occasion when product-page information confused a buyer.
2. What information did the merchant inspect? Who could confirm it?
3. How was the page changed, and how long did it take? Distinguish recollection from measured time.
4. Which proposed facts would the participant refuse to publish without stronger evidence?
5. Ask the merchant to inspect the synthetic Commuter One scenario. Can they explain which issues are content, quality or fulfillment issues?
6. Ask whether this task occurs often enough to justify repeated use. Do not lead with a price.

Collect only consented, de-identified notes. Do not publish business records without separate authorization.

## Two task tests

At least one participant should have merchant or agency experience; neither should be the primary developer. Give each participant a page-repair task and a matched manual task in alternating order. Capture start/end time, wrong facts, help requests, abandoned steps and ability to restore a prior page. Report individual observations; two participants do not establish market-wide effects.
